About

Problem first, then code.

I'm an engineer who starts with the problem, not the code. Before writing a line, I work to understand what a product actually needs to do for the people and the business using it, then design the simplest solution that solves it well. I bring a strong analytical mindset to that: breaking ambiguous problems into clear parts, weighing trade-offs, and choosing the approach that will hold up as things grow. My focus is on solutions that are effective, scalable, and efficient: not just software that works today, but software that keeps working as it scales.

Most of my work lives where design and engineering meet. I currently co-lead a multi-brand design system at a telecommunications company, where I own its architecture: the design tokens, patterns, and components that keep web and mobile products consistent across several brands. Lately I've focused on integrating AI into how software gets built, in a way that's practical rather than ornamental. I move comfortably between leading and building: I've served as Scrum Master and Product Owner while staying hands-on across frontend, backend, cloud, and architecture.
